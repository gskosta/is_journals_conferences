About this directory & how to read the evidence codes

A worldwide directory covering core Information Systems (IS), regional and national IS meetings, specialist conferences, AIS research workshops, information-systems engineering, and selected adjacent research communities.

The month column is a planning estimate — the recurring month where editions agree, or the observed window where they differ. It is not an arithmetic average. Dates refer to the initial full research-paper submission unless a row says otherwise. Always confirm the current call before submitting.

JUFO level comes from Finland's official Publication Forum export (jufo-rest.csc.fi), shown only for the 40 venues with a JUFO ID (0–3, higher is more selective). CORE rank comes from the ICORE conference portal (portal.core.edu.au, CORE2023 source). ERA rank (A/B/C, 2010) and Qualis rank (A1/A2/B1–B5/C, 2012) come from the conferenceranks.com historical archive of the Australian ERA and Brazilian CAPES Qualis assessments. Matches were verified by title, not acronym alone, since several acronyms in this directory (e.g. WISE, WITS, AOM) are shared by unrelated venues. Most IS-discipline venues here fall outside these computer-science/software-engineering-oriented rankings and are not assessed — a missing badge means "not checked or not in that ranking", not "unranked". MSAR (2014) was also requested, but conferenceranks.com's MSAR data file is no longer available (404), so it is not included.
